## DAPO generation and filtering cost

Each candidate prompt group has 16 independently sampled trajectories. A group
is heterogeneous when 1–15 of its 16 SQL results are correct. All-incorrect and
all-correct groups are discarded by the online binary-result filter, even when
other reward components differ. Each update retains 32 mixed groups.

The async sampler drains requests already in flight after enough groups qualify.
Additional mixed groups are discarded as surplus rather than reused after the
policy changes. The upstream metric names end in `samples`, but these two
filtering counters count **prompt groups**.

| Update | Generated groups | Generated trajectories | Homogeneous groups discarded | Surplus mixed groups discarded | Retained trajectories |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 132 | 2,112 | 72 | 28 | 512 |
| 2 | 165 | 2,640 | 88 | 45 | 512 |
| 3 | 152 | 2,432 | 83 | 37 | 512 |
| 4 | 110 | 1,760 | 59 | 19 | 512 |
| 5 | 165 | 2,640 | 106 | 27 | 512 |
| 6 | 181 | 2,896 | 123 | 26 | 512 |
| 7 | 214 | 3,424 | 154 | 28 | 512 |

Across these seven updates: **17,904 generated trajectories; 3,584 retained
(20.02% retention)**. Counts are reconstructed as
`(32 + homogeneous_discarded + mixed_surplus_discarded) × 16`; no rollout-failure
evictions were recorded in these updates. Reducing speculative surplus is a
future throughput opportunity; the active recipe has not been changed.

The response budget is cumulative across the multi-turn trajectory, including
retained tool observations and formatting; it is not a fresh 8K budget per turn.

The 1,039 training prompts comprise 561 heterogeneous screening prompts plus
478 randomly selected prompts from the 4,783 all-correct groups (10%, rounded
down; seed 20261029). Screening used all 6,601 original training prompts × 16
base-model rollouts at temperature 1.2 and an 8K response budget. Online DAPO
filtering still applies when selected prompts are sampled again.

Training prompts use bare schemas without retrieved few-shot examples. The
validation prompts include three train-derived few-shot examples and enriched
schema information. Run-specific parquets change only the system budget text
from 4,096 to 8,192 relative to their selected/native sources.
